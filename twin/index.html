What the data says (sensitivity at the selected distance)

ChangeΔ apparent T

From the 78-photo ANOVA on the cooling length (R² = 0.71): fan ≫ nozzle temperature > spool speed (spool speed barely significant, p = 0.05). Fans shorten the cooling length by ≈20 % (Mid) and ≈19 % (On), at all three nozzle temperatures. A hotter nozzle keeps the fiber hotter over the first centimeters. Photo-to-photo repeatability of the cooling length ≈ 4 %.

Model: a semi-physical curve T = T_b + a·exp(−x/L) + c whose three coefficients depend log-linearly on nozzle temperature, spooler speed and fan level (12 parameters). It is fitted to the photos, so don’t read it outside 90–110 °C and 20–40 rpm. Spool radius 0.765 cm; camera scale calibrated per photo from the 2.5 cm heater width. Photos taken against a black background; diameters measured by the FrED camera. At 20 rpm the spool speed oscillates (about 6–34 rpm), so the 20 rpm profiles are the least reliable.
